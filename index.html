Our story

A Little Love in Every Stitch.

Hooked with Love is a handmade crafts business offering crochet products, fuzzy crafts, keychains, and personalized gifts. We believe small, thoughtful creations can make everyday moments feel special.

Our Mission

To create thoughtful handmade gifts that celebrate creativity and personal connection.

Our Vision

To make charming, personalized crafts accessible to more people.

Our Values

Creativity, personalization, affordability, and care in every detail.

What We Create

Crochet keychains, flowers, fuzzy crafts, and personalized gifts.
